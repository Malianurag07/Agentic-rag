## Payment methods
aliases: how to pay; accepted cards; paypal; pay with; bank transfer; credit card
description: Which ways of paying are accepted.
We accept credit cards, debit cards and PayPal. Bank transfers are available for business accounts only.

## Invoices
aliases: receipt; vat invoice; tax invoice; download invoice; proof of purchase
description: Getting invoices and receipts for orders.
Invoices are available in the account page under Billing within 24 hours of payment. Business customers can add a VAT number before ordering.

## Failed payment
aliases: card declined; payment not going through; payment error; card keeps getting declined; transaction failed
description: What happens when a payment does not go through.
If a payment fails, check the card details and the available balance. The order is held for 24 hours before it is cancelled. Contact your bank if the card keeps being declined.

## Billing dispute
aliases: double charged; charged twice; chargeback; wrong charge; unknown charge; duplicate charge; charged the same order twice
description: Problems with the amount or number of charges.
If you were charged twice, contact support with both transaction dates. Duplicate charges are reversed within 5 business days. Opening a chargeback with your bank first can delay the review.
