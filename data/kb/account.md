## Reset password
aliases: forgot password; cannot log in; login problem; locked out; change my password
description: Getting back into your account.
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. The reset email arrives within 10 minutes and the link expires after 1 hour.

## Delete account
aliases: close account; remove my data; erase my data; delete personal data; leave the service
description: Closing an account and removing personal data.
Account deletion removes personal data within 30 days. Open orders must be completed first.
