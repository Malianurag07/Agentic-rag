## Damage refund
aliases: damaged item; broken item; arrived smashed; item damaged on arrival; money back for damaged goods; cracked; shattered
description: Refunds for items that arrive broken or damaged.
Items that arrive damaged or broken can be refunded in full within 7 days of delivery. Send a clear photo of the damage and the packaging to support. A refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the photo is approved. Damage caused by the customer after delivery is not covered.

## Late delivery refund
aliases: order not delivered; package late; delayed order; has not arrived; never arrived on time; waiting too long for my order; reshipment
description: Refund or free reshipment when delivery takes too long.
If an order has not arrived after 10 business days, the customer may choose a full refund or a free reshipment. Shipping fees are refunded as well. Requests must be made within 30 days of the order date.

## Change of mind refund
aliases: return; changed my mind; no longer want; return an item; send back; restocking fee
description: Returns of undamaged items because the customer changed their mind.
Change of mind returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. A 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund. Items must be unused and in the original packaging.

## Subscription refund
aliases: cancel subscription; monthly plan; annual plan; cancel plan; charged for renewal; membership
description: Refunds for monthly and annual subscription plans.
Monthly plans can be refunded within 7 days of the charge. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the charge. After that period the plan stays active until the end of the paid term.
